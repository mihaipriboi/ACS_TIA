# Lab overview

[laborator TIA.pptx](laborator%20TIA.pptx) — introductory lab slides covering the lab syllabus and grading.

## Syllabus

1. Organizational details
2. Data processing
3. Regression
4. Classification
5. K-means
6. Decision trees
7. Conclusions and final discussion

## Grading

- **50 points** total for the lab component:
  - **20 points** for in-lab activity (4 points × 5 working labs)
  - **30 points** for homework (15 points × 2 checkpoints)
- **Minimum to pass the lab:** 25 points
- Attendance at **at least 4** working labs is mandatory.
- Homework must be uploaded to Moodle on time.
- Labs cannot be made up during the semester.
- The first and last labs have no graded activity.
